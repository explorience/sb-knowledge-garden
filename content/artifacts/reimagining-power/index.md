The Reimagining Power Project represents an 18-month journey exploring how web3 technologies might redistribute rather than replicate existing power structures in philanthropy and social impact. What began as technology exploration evolved into fundamental questions about participation, ownership, and collective decision-making.

## Project Overview

In the face of multiple crises, systemic inequities, and entrenched power imbalances, philanthropy stands at a pivotal moment. The Reimagining Power Project investigates how emerging web3 technologies can be leveraged to fundamentally alter the structures and systems that perpetuate power imbalances, moving beyond incremental change toward bold, systemic transformation.

## Key Themes

- **Power Redistribution**: Examining how to transfer and share power meaningfully rather than merely discussing it
- **Decentralized Governance**: Practical experimentation with autonomous cells coordinating within shared purpose
- **Stakeholder Participation**: Enabling authentic participation beyond advisory committees
- **Web3 Solutions**: Leveraging blockchain, DAOs, and smart contracts to address systemic challenges

## Real-World Implementation

The project supported three organizations through governance transformation:

- **All In For Sport**: Sports coordination moving from centralized control to distributed decision-making
- **Institute for Community Sustainability**: Decentralizing governance into local communities
- **Equality Fund**: International grantmaking with reduced bureaucracy and increased efficiency

These experiments achieved measurable improvements in participation, innovation, and impact, documented in our case studies.
