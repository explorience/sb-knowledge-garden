**Governance for Better Futures** is a conversation series exploring how the nature of governance is evolving, and inquiring into the governance we need to accelerate the arrival of better futures.

## Series Overview

SuperBenefit DAO convened a series of Twitter Spaces to probe perspectives on governance from practitioners bringing diverse and complementary viewpoints - including experience from DAOs, other decentralized networks, the co-operative movement, Indigenous cultures, purpose-led business, and civic and systems innovation.

We are specifically interested in how Web3 technologies are enabling new models of governance towards these ends, and their potential to combine with existing practices, perspectives, and wisdom.

## Three Conversations

Over three conversations, we explored perspectives on the essential functions and attributes of governance, and the design implications for governance arrangements that seek to foster decentralisation, pluralism, and regeneration.

### Conversation Themes

1. **Meta-governance**: What is it and what's its potential? Exploring governance beyond individual organizations to systems-level coordination.

2. **DAO-to-DAO Cooperation**: What are we seeing and learning from inter-DAO cooperation? Understanding collaboration across decentralized networks.

3. **New and Old**: What can Web3 learn from and contribute to existing governance perspectives and practices? Bridging emerging and established governance wisdom.

## Key Insights

The series examined:

- The evolving nature of governance in decentralized contexts
- How web3 technologies enable new coordination mechanisms
- The potential for combining new technological capabilities with traditional governance wisdom
- Meta-governance and governance in a systems context
- Practical considerations for fostering decentralization and pluralism
