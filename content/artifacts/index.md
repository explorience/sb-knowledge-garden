Welcome to the SuperBenefit artifacts repository! This section contains the finished products of our collaborative efforts, showcasing refined knowledge, insights, and frameworks for pro-social innovation.

## Featured Releases

Our knowledge artifacts are organized into **releases** - curated collections of related work around specific projects, initiatives, and thematic series. Each release brings together multiple artifact types (articles, guides, patterns, studies, playbooks) into a coherent presentation of a complete body of work.

### [Reimagining Power Project](artifacts/reimagining-power/index.md)

Exploring how web3 technologies can redistribute power, mobilize capital, and transform systems toward more equitable outcomes in philanthropy and social impact. Through 18 months of real-world experimentation with three partner organizations, this project demonstrates how decentralized governance patterns can achieve measurable improvements in participation, innovation, and impact.

**Includes**: Articles, playbooks, and case studies from partner organizations

### [DAO Primitives Framework](artifacts/dao-primitives/index.md)

A comprehensive framework for designing decentralized organizations as purpose-aligned networks of small autonomous teams. Provides dimensional lenses (Phase, Scale, State), organizational primitives (DAOs, Cells, Roles, Tasks), implementation guides, and a patterns library for building effective decentralized systems.

**Includes**: Framework documentation, implementation guides, patterns, and article series

### [Governance for Better Futures](artifacts/governance-futures/index.md)

A conversation series exploring how governance is evolving and what models we need to accelerate the arrival of better futures. Examines perspectives from DAOs, co-operative movements, Indigenous cultures, and civic innovation on meta-governance, inter-DAO cooperation, and the intersection of new and traditional governance wisdom.

**Includes**: Article series from Twitter Spaces conversations
